A LITTLE CARE GOES A LONG WAY

Keep your sparkle beautiful

Simple habits to help care for your favourite pieces.

Store with care

Keep jewellery in a clean, dry place and separate pieces to help avoid scratches and tangles.

Keep it dry

As a general precaution, remove jewellery before bathing, swimming or applying perfume.

Clean gently

Use a soft, clean cloth to gently wipe your jewellery after wearing it.

Handle thoughtfully

Put on jewellery after getting ready and handle delicate pieces with care.
